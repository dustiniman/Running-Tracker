I chose this topic because I really enjoy running, but I just got into it last year and I found that most apps or trackers give bare minmum information to you after a run unless you pay a subscription, and I do not like the idea of a healthier life being stuck behind a pay wall.
* Background on the topic
Running Statistics are extremely useful to help everyone train more efficiently and safer. Anyone from a high level athlete to someone that just feels like getting in shape can find that feedback and the ability to track progress helps boost future progress.
* How this program helps you or another party
The first thing my program does is it organizes all data into records. Also, it evaluates overall pace and gives feedback based on your outcome.
* How to use your program
Open the program and compile files and make sure Run.java and Main.java are in the same directory. Then, run the Main class. Review results that show your history of calculated running statistics.
